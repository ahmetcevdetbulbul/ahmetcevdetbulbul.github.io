GateWeaver — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how GateWeaver (the "App"), published by Ludexa Studio ("we", "us"), handles information when you play. We do not operate user accounts or our own servers, and the App does not ask for your name, email address, or other contact details.

Information Stored on Your Device

Your game progress, scores, and settings are stored locally on your device. This information is not transmitted to us. It remains on your device until you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, which operate under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): The App displays ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app details, and ad interaction data to deliver, measure, and secure ads. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization in your Android settings (typically under Settings → Google → Ads). Learn more: How Google uses data in ads.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect or store your personal data on our own servers, we have nothing to delete on our side. To remove locally stored data, clear the App's storage or uninstall the App. Data held by Google (such as advertising data) can be managed through your Google Account and Android settings.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example when new features or services are added to the App. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
